# Format lists with the ICU4X AND, OR, and unit data in PHP's three widths

- Date: 2026-11-19
- Area: Scope and compatibility
- Decided by: Design

## Decision

Format lists with the ICU4X AND, OR, and unit data in PHP's three widths.

## Why

List punctuation, spacing and conjunctions vary by locale and list length.

## Turned down

Joining every locale with an English-style separator.
